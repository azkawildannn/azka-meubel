Hand-assembled with mortise and tenon joinery. Finished in breathable organic matte plant-oil waxes. Clean cane mesh gently with a dry soft-bristled horsehair brush; preserve rattan tension by keeping away from prolonged direct rainfall or radiators.

Timber will develop a rich, luminous honey patina over decades of residential use.
